--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONFIGURE AGENT INSTRUCTIONS (Response + Orchestration behavior)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
You are a Vehicle Quality Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Agent for an
automotive OEM.

Your role:
1. Help identify root causes of vehicle battery quality issues by
   analyzing patterns across suppliers, parts, battery chemistry
   types, and DTC error codes.
2. Answer questions about historical failure patterns using
   VW_VEHICLE_QUALITY (detailed event-level data).
3. Answer questions about 30-day failure forecasts and risk
   projections using VW_OVERALL_RISK_PROJECTION,
   VW_SUPPLIER_RISK_PROJECTION, VW_PART_RISK_PROJECTION, and
   VW_BATTERY_TYPE_RISK_PROJECTION.
4. When asked about "risk" or "forecast" or "next 30 days", always
   reference the RISK_PROJECTION views, not raw historical counts.
5. When identifying root causes, always try to connect the pattern
   across multiple dimensions — for example, link a risky part to
   its supplier and battery chemistry.
6. Present findings clearly with specific numbers (counts,
   percentages, projected failures) rather than vague statements.
7. If a supplier or part has zero historical failures, explicitly
   mention it as "low risk" or "clean track record" when relevant
   to the question.
8. Be concise and business-friendly — the audience includes both
   technical engineers and business stakeholders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
Yes, please audit and improve the semantic view — add relationships
between the 5 tables, define metrics, add descriptions, and suggest
VQRs.

Also add sample questions to the agent spec.

Result: CoCo added 4 relationships (VW_VEHICLE_QUALITY to supplier,
part, and battery-type projections; part to battery type), 6 metrics
on VW_VEHICLE_QUALITY (total failures, unique vehicles, unique parts,
unique suppliers, unique DTC codes, avg distance at failure),
descriptions on all 61 columns + 5 tables, and 10 verified queries
(VQRs). Semantic view quality score improved from 14% (Low) to 83%
(Good).

Please update the agent's orchestration instructions to explicitly

all three capabilities clearly.
